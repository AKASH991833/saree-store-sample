Do I need to create an account to order?

No. There is no login, password or OTP anywhere. You pick what you like and send the order on WhatsApp - the shop confirms availability and the final total in chat.

How do I pay?

Exactly as you do at the shop today - cash or UPI - settled with the shop directly on WhatsApp, at pickup or delivery. No online payment is taken on this sample site.

What about delivery and exchange?

Policies are not set in this sample - they will be published with the live store. For now, shop pickup needs no address, and delivery details are settled in chat.
